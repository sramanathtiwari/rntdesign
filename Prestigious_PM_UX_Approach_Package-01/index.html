The short version

What I noticed, what I would do, and what I need from you

Read this part if you only have a minute.

What I noticed

  • The brand, services and leadership content are a solid base.
  • The page is long, and several sections repeat similar ideas.
  • Enquiry actions compete, and some text is small or low in contrast.

What I would do

  • Keep 7 sections on the landing page.
  • Move the detail of 6 sections to inner pages.
  • Give visitors one clear next step, with consistent, accessible components.

What I need from you

  • The main audience and main action for Prestigious PM.
  • Whether the logo and business name stay as they are.
  • Any analytics or feedback you already have.
